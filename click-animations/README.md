## How it works

Every left-click starts the selected effect at the click point.
Each effect has 48 frames and lasts about 0.77 seconds.
Impulse creates a light wave, Orbit creates three rotating arcs, and Particles creates ten rising points of light.

## Installation

AutoHotkey v2 is required.
Keep `click-animations.ahk`, `impulse.bgra`, `orbit.bgra`, and `particle.bgra` together in this folder, then start `click-animations.ahk`.

## Usage

On the numeric keypad, hold `4`, `5`, or `6` and press `+` to select Impulse, Orbit, or Particles. Alternatively, right-click the tray icon and choose an effect.
The shortcuts can be changed as required in `click-animations.ahk`.
